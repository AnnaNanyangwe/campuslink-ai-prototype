# CampusLink
### 🌐 Live Prototype
[Visit CampusLink](https://campuslink-7lks.onrender.com?utm)
CampusLink is an AI-powered university marketplace and opportunity platform designed to connect students, lecturers, and the wider campus community in one digital space.**

The platform enables students to advertise products and services, showcase their skills, discover projects, internships, research opportunities, and campus jobs, and connect with other students for collaboration.

Lecturers and university staff can also share opportunities, find students with relevant skills, and connect with students who can contribute to projects, research, or other academic activities.

CampusLink uses AI-assisted matching to help students discover opportunities that are relevant to their skills, interests, and academic background.

By bringing these activities into one university-focused platform, CampusLink can help students turn their skills into opportunities, support student entrepreneurship, improve collaboration across the campus community, and make university opportunities easier to discover.
